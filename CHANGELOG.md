# Changelog

Copero99's version numbers go up in the middle for new features and at the end for fixes.

## 1.0.0

The first public release.

### Three ways to play
- Every career starts with a choice, made before the first season and kept for the whole career.
- Boost rolls a hidden talent for your player: one of 11 levels from Journeyman to The Chosen One, a career style, perks, a role trait and playing traits. Copero plays the career out, and your scouting report stays sealed until you retire. You get one free reroll.
- Sandbox lets you set OVR 99 or your own growth curve, pick perks and traits by hand, get better club offers and protection from injuries and bans, retire whenever you like and go back in time.
- Vanilla is Copero exactly as it is, except that you can turn down a loan.
- Only Vanilla careers unlock achievements on your Copero account.

### The Living world
- Under the game card, your club's whole season plays out: the league table, the cup run, the continental campaign and the Club World Cup. It always matches the trophies Copero gives you.
- Your country's caps, call-ups and tournaments are shown too, and you can look back at any earlier season.
- Club and country reputations rise and fall with results, so superteams form and fade and small clubs have the odd surprise season.
- Clubs and national teams start with their real managers, taken from Wikipedia, who get sacked, hired and poached. Later in a career, some of today's star players turn up as managers.
- Promotion and relegation follow each country's real rules, play-offs included. A promotion is celebrated with a green "Promoted" arrow.
- The Club World Cup is played every 4 years, only by clubs that qualified.

### Awards
- The Ballon d'Or is voted against the season's real stars, and the jury's mood changes from year to year. Your placing shows as a medal every season, and goalkeepers can be nominated like real ones.
- League goals in stronger leagues count for more in the Golden Boot race, and goalkeepers compete for the Golden Glove.

### Your career
- From your early-to-mid 30s, a Retire card joins your decisions when your body, the bench or your drop from your best says it's time. Press it to choose how you go: retire now, play a farewell season at your club, have a last dance at the club where you started, or go home to a top club in your own country.
- There's no age limit. Playing on past your body costs OVR, games and offers, and the career only ends by itself after a big fall from your best.
- Transfer windows bring clubs that need your position, release clauses and the odd last adventure abroad. Fierce rivals won't sign each other's stars, and the same clubs seldom come back window after window.
- Your games follow your club's real season, so a starter at a big club plays 55 to 60 games.
- Club fit, form, contracts, captaincy and bans with consequences all shape a career.

### Hall of Fame
- Finished careers can be saved as FIFA-style cards, from Bronze up to Icon and National Hero, with your shirt and number, the titles and traits you earned, every club you played for and a table of every season.

### Look and feel
- Themes and fonts restyle the whole site. The default theme takes your club's colours and puts its crest and slow waves in those colours beside the game.
- Dice buttons on the name screen pick a random country, position, shirt number and foot.
- Copero's achievements window shows how far your current career is towards each achievement.
- Copero99 works on computers, tablets and phones, where the panel opens as a sheet at the bottom of the screen.
